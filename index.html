US 95 Passing and No-Passing Zones on Rural Two-Lane, Two-Way Highways

University of Nevada, Reno · 08/2026 – Present

  • Proof of concept
  • Telematics
  • Passing zones

Research problem

  • Does the current passing zone guidance give drivers enough distance and time on high-speed rural two-lane roads?
  • How do passing zone frequency, distribution, and length, taken together, affect safety on rural two-lane, two-way roads?

Data

  • Telematic trajectory data.
  • NDOT crash data to back up the observed data.

Method

  • Compare the observed data with MUTCD, AASHTO, and NCHRP 605 criteria.
  • Check whether the existing markings meet the MUTCD criteria.

Proof of concept result

Observed passing lengths far exceed the design passing sight distance, across 1,972 passing maneuvers.
